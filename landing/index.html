How it works

Most families are set up in an afternoon.

  1. Add your students and subjects

    Create your family account, then add each child and the subjects you teach.

  2. Plan the work

    Add assignments or a curriculum once. tally schedules the days for you.

  3. Let them check it off

    Kids work through their day while you watch progress from your dashboard.

One tablet or one each

Kids don’t need their own email or password.

  • Student ModeHand your own device to a child. It shows only their view until you enter your family PIN.
  • Pair their own deviceEnter a one-time code on a child’s tablet or computer, and it’s theirs. No account setup.
  • Or a login of their ownOlder students can have their own email and password whenever you’re ready.
