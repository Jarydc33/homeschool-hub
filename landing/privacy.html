Privacy Policy

Effective November 16, 2026

The short version

  • A parent creates the family account and decides what goes in it, including everything about their children.
  • We use your information only to run tally for your family.
  • We don’t sell it, show ads, or share it for marketing. There are no tracking or analytics tools in the app.
  • Each family’s information is kept apart from every other family’s.
  • You can ask us to see, correct, export or delete your family’s information at any time.

Who we are

tally homeschool (“tally”, “we”, “us”) is a planner and record keeper for homeschooling families, at tallyhomeschool.com and app.tallyhomeschool.com. This policy covers the website, the app on the web, and the iPhone and Android apps when they’re released. Questions go to hello@tallyhomeschool.com.

Who tally is for

Accounts are made by parents or guardians, who must be adults. Children use tally only through the family account their parent controls: a parent adds each child, and decides whether the child uses a parent’s device (Student Mode), a device the parent pairs with a one-time code, or a login of their own.

What we collect

About parents

  • Your email address and password, for signing in. Passwords are handled by our sign-in provider and stored only in scrambled (hashed) form; we never see them.
  • Your family name and time zone.
  • A 4-digit family PIN, if you set one, stored only in hashed form.
  • Messages you send us through the app’s feedback form, with your email address so we can reply.

About your children

Only what a parent enters, or what a child records while using the family account:

  • Name, and optionally grade level and birth year.
  • An email address, only if a parent gives a child a login of their own.
  • Schoolwork: subjects, assignments, curriculum lessons, completion, notes, scores and grades.
  • Reading (books, pages and dates), P.E. activities and minutes, and family events.
  • Streaks and badges, which are worked out from the schoolwork above.

About devices

  • Your sign-in is kept in your browser or device storage so you stay signed in.
  • A paired device keeps a random key in its storage; we keep only a hashed copy, with when it was paired and last used.
  • Like most websites, our hosting providers record technical details of each request, such as IP address, browser type and time, in server logs used for security and fixing problems.

We don’t use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies, and we don’t collect location, contacts, photos or anything else from your device.

Children’s privacy

We take children’s privacy seriously, including under the U.S. Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

  • A parent provides and controls all information about a child. We don’t ask children for personal information, and a child can’t create an account on their own.
  • A child’s information is used only to run tally for their family: showing their work, recording what they finish, and showing their progress to them and their parents.
  • It’s never used for advertising, never sold, and never shared outside the service providers listed below.
  • Parents can see and change everything about their children in the app, archive a child at any time, and ask us to delete a child’s information completely by emailing hello@tallyhomeschool.com.

If you believe a child has given us information without a parent’s permission, please email us and we’ll delete it.

How we use information

  • To run tally: showing each person the right family’s plans, records and progress.
  • To send account emails you ask for, such as confirming your email, inviting a student and resetting a password.
  • To answer your feedback and questions.
  • To keep tally secure, prevent misuse and fix problems.

We don’t sell personal information, share it for advertising, or use it to build profiles of you or your children.

Who we share it with

Only the service providers that run tally for us, under their own privacy and security commitments, and only as far as each needs:

We may also share information if the law requires it, to protect someone’s safety, or as part of a sale or merger of tally, in which case this policy would continue to apply to your information.

How we protect it

  • All connections to tally are encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Every request is checked against your family, so one family can never see another’s information.
  • Passwords, the family PIN and device keys are stored only in hashed form.
  • Leaving Student Mode or unpairing a device needs the family PIN, and repeated wrong tries are locked out for a while.

No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information, and we’ll tell affected families promptly if a breach ever affects their information.

How long we keep it

We keep your family’s information while your account is open, so your records build up over the years. Archiving a student, subject or assignment hides it but keeps its history. When you delete your account in the app’s Settings, your family’s information and logins are deleted straight away; if you ask us by email instead, we do it within 30 days. Copies in our backups are removed as those backups expire.

Your choices

  • See and change it: everything you and your children enter can be viewed and edited in the app.
  • Get a copy: email us for a copy of your family’s information.
  • Delete it: delete your whole account at any time under Settings → Delete Account in the app. To delete just one child’s information, email us; we’ll confirm the request comes from the account’s parent first.

Depending on where you live, you may have further rights over your information; we’ll honor them. Email hello@tallyhomeschool.com for any of these.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date at the top. If a change affects how we use your information in an important way, we’ll tell you by email or in the app before it takes effect.

Contact

Email hello@tallyhomeschool.com with any question about privacy or this policy.
